Hazards & Tricky Water — 11 places

Lake-wide shallow water and submerged stump fields

TWRA: about 68% of Reelfoot Lake is 3.0 feet deep or less and filled with stump fields that can hamper navigation. Go slow — submerged stumps and standing timber throughout; spring wind can make open water dangerous.

Dam tailwater / forebay — sudden water releases, strong currents

TVA hazardous waters: this dam can release large amounts of water with little warning. Currents can be hard to escape, even in a motorized boat, and dangers extend far below the dam. Leave the areas upstream and downstream of the dam when warning horns, strobes or signs activate. Never anchor below a dam.
