Safety & Emergency

The first-call list every boater in America needs — the same one we run in Tampa Bay, good on any water in the country.

First call

Emergency contacts

Life-threatening emergency: 911

Distress on the water: VHF Channel 16 — say “Mayday” three times, then your boat name, your position, what’s wrong, and how many people are aboard. The Coast Guard monitors Channel 16 around the clock.

Poison Control: 800-222-1222

Fuel or oil spill: National Response Center 800-424-8802

Towing (not an emergency): TowBoatUS dispatch 800-391-4869 · Sea Tow members — dispatch through the Sea Tow app or your local franchise.

Before you go: file a float plan — tell someone on shore where you’re headed and when you’ll be back, and make sure someone aboard can run the boat and the radio.

How It Grows

1 · One region at a time.
No nationwide data dump. Each region launches only when its map, events and prices are verified the local way.
2 · Free for boaters, always.
Sponsors and featured local businesses pay. Boaters, event listings and rental listings never do.
3 · Middleman only.
We connect boaters to local businesses and take no bookings, no sales, no cut of anyone's money.
Own a marine business? When your region opens, your free listing comes first — sponsors get the featured spots. That's the whole model, same as Tampa Bay.
