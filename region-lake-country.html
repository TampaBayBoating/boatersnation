🌊 Lake Country

North Georgia's Corps lakes, the Missouri Ozarks and the Texas Highland Lakes. Built only from records that passed the Nov 6, 2026 verification pass; coverage is partial — the gaps are named, not filled.

Georgia, Missouri and Texas inland lakes — verified coverage: 201 pins that passed check + independent recheck (ramps 190 · hazards 11). Coverage is partial: Texas boat ramps are not pinned yet (the official Texas layer publishes county codes instead of ramp names, and a name is never invented), there are no fuel pins (every fuel claim tested came back unstated by an official source), and records still being verified are not pinned. J. Strom Thurmond Lake is not in this zone's census and is not covered.

Boater's Map — Lake Country (verified pins only)

Every pin on this page passed this zone's verification pass: boat ramps corroborated by dual-source agreement (state agency records + OpenStreetMap, 250 m rule), and hazard pins anchored at each lake's dam carrying the managing authority's warning text quoted verbatim — the pin marks the dam, and its popup states that the warning is lake-wide with no specific location. Records that failed, conflicted, or carry a code instead of a name are NOT pinned — they sit in the zone's exception queue (595 entries) for human recheck. Texas waters carry hazard pins only for now: the official Texas ramp layer publishes county codes, not ramp names, and nothing was invented to fill the gap.
