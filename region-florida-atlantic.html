Every state has its own FWC. These are the official state agencies for this region — registration, boating laws, fishing licenses and state-run launch lists, straight from the source. The state sites carry the official rules.

Florida Fish & Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC)

Florida's FWC — boating laws & safety, the safety-education card, fishing licenses and the state's own boat-ramp finder. One job is NOT FWC's: registration and titling are issued through your county Tax Collector's office, under the FL Dept. of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles — FWC's own regulations page states this.

Register a boat
Vessel Titling & Registrations — FLHSMVTitles and registrations run through your local county Tax Collector's office; a vessel must be registered and numbered within 30 days of purchase.
